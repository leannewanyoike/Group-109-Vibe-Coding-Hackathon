About CloudReady

The problem

Many learners preparing for AZ-900 practise questions but don't understand their mistakes or know what to study next.

How CloudReady helps

  1. Answer a practice question.
  2. See the right answer, why it's right, and a real-life example.
  3. Track your mastery in each of the three AZ-900 domains.
  4. Follow a recommendation to your weakest topic.
  5. Use the Study Buddy to understand the concepts you missed.

Built with Web Foundations skills

CloudReady uses only what our team learned in the Web Foundations course: semantic HTML pages, CSS with Flexbox, Grid and media queries, and vanilla JavaScript with the DOM, form validation, localStorage and fetch. There are no frameworks and no build step.

Important

CloudReady is an unofficial study tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft. All questions are original practice items, not real exam questions. Your readiness estimate is based only on practice in this app and does not predict your exam result. Always check content against the official AZ-900 study guide.
